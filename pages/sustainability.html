The 17 Sustainable Development Goals

Adopted by all United Nations Member States in 2015, the SDGs are an urgent call for action. They are the blueprint to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all.

Goal 1: No Poverty

End poverty in all its forms everywhere, ensuring everyone has equal resources and opportunities.

Learn More

Goal 2: Zero Hunger

End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture.

Learn More

Goal 3: Good Health and Well-Being

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages, reducing mortality and fighting diseases.

Learn More

Goal 4: Quality Education

Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.

Learn More

Goal 5: Gender Equality

Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls, eliminating discrimination and violence.

Learn More

Goal 6: Clean Water and Sanitaion

Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all.

Learn More

Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all.

Learn More

Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promote sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment.

Learn More

Goal 9: Industry,Innovation and Infrastructure

Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization and foster innovation.

Learn More

Goal 10: Reduce Inequalities

Reduce inequality within and among countries by empowering and promoting social inclusion.

Learn More

Goal 11:Sustainable Cities and Communities

Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable.

Learn More

Goal 12: Responsible Consumption And Production

Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns by reducing waste and promoting recycling.

Learn More

Goal 13: Climate Action

Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts through policy and adaptation.

Learn More

Goal 14: Life Below Water

Conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas and marine resources for sustainable development.

Goal 15: Life on Land

Protect, restore and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems and halt biodiversity loss.

Goal 16: Peace,Justice & Strong Institutions

Promote peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development and access to justice for all.
